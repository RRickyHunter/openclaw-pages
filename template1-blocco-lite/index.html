Most people try to solve this with more knowledge. Another book. Another course. Another framework.

The real issue

You don't have a knowledge problem.

You have an internal state problem.

Your nervous system is running unexamined programs that were installed years ago.

How SMC Mentoring Works

This isn't a course you passively consume. It's a structured journey you actively move through—designed to transform your relationship with your internal experience.

The path

Each session is designed to build on the last. We start with awareness—learning to see the patterns that have been running you. Then we move into intervention—targeted practices that create real shifts in how you relate to your internal state.

This isn't theory. It's experiential work. You'll leave each session with direct experiences of what it feels like to operate from a different internal state.

Between sessions, you'll have targeted practices designed to rewire the patterns we're working with. These aren't generic meditations you could find on YouTube.

Support around the work

They're precise interventions—rooted in somatics, trauma-informed work, and nervous system regulation—that meet you exactly where you are and create the conditions for real, lasting change.

You're not doing this alone. You'll be part of a small, carefully curated group of people walking the same path—committed to doing the work, not just talking about it.
