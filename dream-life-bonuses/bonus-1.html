Find Your Profitable Idea

The 3-Step System to Discover What Business to Start

Step 1: Inventory Your Skills (15 minutes)

What are you ALREADY good at?

List everything you've done in the last 5 years:

  • Jobs you've had
  • Tasks people ask you to help with
  • Hobbies you enjoy
  • Things you learn faster than others
  • Problems you've solved for yourself
The Sweet Spot Formula:
What you're good at + What people pay for = Your business

Quick Exercise:

  1. Write down 10 things you're good at
  2. Circle the 3 that excite you most
  3. Put a star next to ones people have asked you to help with

Step 2: Find the Money (20 minutes)

Where are people ALREADY spending money?

Look for:

  • Facebook groups with people complaining about problems
  • Amazon bestsellers in categories you know
  • Services your friends pay for
  • Things you wish existed
The $1,000 Test:
Can you find 10 people who would pay $100 for this? That's $1,000. That's a business.

Step 3: Validate in 48 Hours

Don't build. Test first.

  1. Post on social: "I'm thinking of helping people with [problem]. Would anyone be interested?"
  2. Message 10 people directly who have this problem
  3. Offer to help ONE person for free in exchange for a testimonial
If 3+ people say yes = Validated idea
If crickets = Pivot and try again

10 Fast-Start Business Ideas

  1. Virtual Assistant - Help entrepreneurs with admin ($25-50/hr)
  2. Social Media Manager - Run accounts for businesses ($500-2k/month)
  3. Freelance Writer - Write content ($0.10-1.00/word)
  4. Online Tutor - Teach via Zoom ($30-100/hr)
  5. Graphic Designer - Create logos, graphics ($50-500/project)
  6. Bookkeeper - Manage business finances ($300-800/month)
  7. Podcast Editor - Edit audio ($50-200/episode)
  8. Website Builder - Simple sites for local businesses ($500-3k)
  9. Video Editor - Edit YouTube/TikTok ($100-500/video)
  10. Course Creator - Teach your skill ($100-500/sale)

Your Action Plan This Week

  • Monday: Complete Step 1 (Skill inventory)
  • Tuesday: Complete Step 2 (Find the money)
  • Wednesday: Pick your top 3 ideas
  • Thursday: Reach out to 10 people (Step 3)
  • Friday: Analyze responses and pick your winner
  • Weekend: Help your first client for free
Remember: Your first business doesn't have to be perfect. It just has to START.
