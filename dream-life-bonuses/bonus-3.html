First $1K Roadmap

From Zero to Your First $1,000 in 30 Days

Week 1: Setup (Days 1-7)

Day 1: Pick Your Service

  • Choose ONE thing from the Side Hustle Starter Kit
  • Write down exactly what you'll offer
  • Set your price (start low: $100-500)

Day 2: Create Your Offer

  • Write a simple description of what you do
  • Create a simple portfolio (3 examples of your work)
  • Set up a simple payment method (PayPal, Venmo)

Day 3: Build Your Presence

  • Create a simple Instagram/LinkedIn profile
  • Post: "I'm helping [target] with [service]. First 3 clients get 50% off!"
  • Add contact info to your bio

Day 4-5: Outreach Blitz

Goal: Message 50 people
  • 20 Facebook group posts
  • 20 direct messages to potential clients
  • 10 emails to local businesses

Message Template:

Hi [Name], I help [target] with [service]. I'm offering 50% off for my first 3 clients. Would you be interested in a quick chat?

Day 6-7: Follow Up

  • Follow up with everyone who didn't respond
  • Book 3-5 discovery calls
  • Prepare what you'll say on calls

Week 2: First Clients (Days 8-14)

Day 8-10: Discovery Calls

Your goal: Book 1-3 clients

Call script (15 minutes):
  1. "Tell me about your business"
  2. "What problems are you facing with [area]?"
  3. "What would it mean to solve this?"
  4. "I can help. Here's what I do..."
  5. "My rate is $[price]. Does that work?"

Day 11-14: Do Amazing Work

  • Over-deliver on your first projects
  • Ask for testimonials
  • Document your process
Goal: Complete 1-3 projects, collect $500-1,000

Week 3: Scale (Days 15-21)

Day 15: Raise Prices

  • New clients: 2x your original price
  • Example: $100 → $200, $250 → $500

Day 16-18: More Outreach

  • Message 50 more people
  • Post results from your first clients
  • Ask for referrals

Day 19-21: Book More Clients

  • Goal: 2-3 more clients at new prices
  • Focus on value, not time
  • Sell packages, not hours

Week 4: Systems (Days 22-30)

Day 22-24: Create Systems

  • Document your process
  • Create templates
  • Automate what you can

Day 25-27: Raise Prices Again

  • New clients: 2x your Week 3 prices
  • Example: $200 → $400, $500 → $1,000

Day 28-30: Hit $1,000

Math:
  • 2 clients at $500 = $1,000
  • 4 clients at $250 = $1,000
  • 1 client at $1,000 = $1,000

Daily Habits for Success

Every morning:
  • Check dream life number
  • Message 5 new potential clients
  • Post content about your service
Every evening:
  • Follow up with 5 people
  • Track your income
  • Celebrate small wins

When You Get Stuck

"No one is responding"
→ Message 2x more people. It's a numbers game.
"They say I'm too expensive"
→ You're not. Find people with bigger problems.
"I don't know what to do"
→ Google it. YouTube it. Figure it out.
"I want to quit"
→ Remember your dream life number.

After You Hit $1,000

  1. Raise prices (2x again)
  2. Get 3 more clients at new price
  3. Hit $3,000/month
  4. Build systems so it's not just you
  5. Scale to your dream number
