9-5 to Dream Life Transition Plan

Quit your job safely without going broke

Months 1-3: Foundation
Build Your Side Income
  • Keep your 9-5 job (steady income)
  • Pick ONE side hustle from the starter kit
  • Work on it 10-15 hours per week (evenings/weekends)
  • Goal: First $1,000 from side income
  • Save 100% of side income as "freedom fund"
Checkpoint: Can you consistently earn $500/month from your side hustle?
Months 4-6: Growth
Replace Half Your Income
  • Still at 9-5, but start mentally checking out
  • Increase side hustle to 20-25 hours per week
  • Raise prices (you have testimonials now)
  • Goal: Side income = 50% of your job salary
  • Freedom fund should be 3-6 months expenses
Checkpoint: Is your side income covering half your monthly expenses?
Months 7-9: Bridge
Match Your Salary
  • Side hustle now equals or exceeds job income
  • Negotiate part-time at your job OR prepare to quit
  • Build systems so side income is more passive
  • Goal: Side income = 100% of job salary
  • Freedom fund: 6+ months expenses saved
Checkpoint: Could you survive 6 months on savings + side income alone?
Month 10: The Jump
Quit Your Job
  • Give 2-week notice (be professional)
  • Side income now your full focus
  • Scale to your dream life number
  • You have 6-month safety net
You're free. Now build your dream life.
Months 11-12: Scale
Hit Your Dream Life Number
  • Full-time on your business
  • Add income streams
  • Outsource tasks you don't like
  • Goal: Reach your dream monthly income
Safety Rules
  • Never quit without 6 months expenses saved
  • Don't quit until side income = 100% of salary
  • Keep job until your business is PROVEN (6+ months consistent income)
  • Have a backup plan (can you get another job if needed?)
